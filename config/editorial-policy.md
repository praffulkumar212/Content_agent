# Editorial policy

- Originality before virality. Do not copy video scripts, thumbnails or a creator's distinct presentation.
- Include source URL and publication/access dates for changeable claims.
- Use an explicit `UNVERIFIED` label when fresh or competitor data could not be retrieved.
- Separate product facts, editorial interpretation, and personal opinion.
- Prefer actionable lessons illustrated by real public examples.
- Do not expose confidential company information.
- Only approved ideas proceed to scripting. No automatic publishing.
